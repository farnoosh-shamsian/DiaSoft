ἠῶθεν δὲ γέροντας ἐπὶ πλέονας καλέσαντες ξεῖνον ἐνὶ μεγάροις ξεινίσσομεν ἠδὲ θεοῖσιν ῥέξομεν ἱερὰ καλά, ἔπειτα δὲ καὶ περὶ πομπῆς μνησόμεθ', ὥς χ' ὁ ξεῖνος ἄνευθε πόνου καὶ ἀνίης πομπῇ ὑφ' ἡμετέρῃ ἣν πατρίδα γαῖαν ἵκηται χαίρων καρπαλίμως, εἰ καὶ μάλα τηλόθεν ἐστί, μηδέ τι μεσσηγύς γε κακὸν καὶ πῆμα πάθῃσι, πρίν γε τὸν ἧς γαίης ἐπιβήμεναι·
"At dawn we will call together still more of the elders, entertain the stranger in our halls and perform fine sacrifices to the gods; and after that we will also give thought to his conveyance, so that the stranger, without toil or distress, under our escort may reach his own native land, rejoicing and swiftly, even if he is from very far away, and suffer no harm or pain in the meantime, before he sets foot on his own land."

Targets are the content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives, temporal/degree adverbs and the relators ἄνευθε, εἰ καί, πρίν) for which an existing FrameNet 1.7 frame fits; particles and connectives (δέ, αὐτάρ, γε, ἄν, μή, ἠδέ, καί as coordinator), the article, personal and possessive pronouns, and the copula ἐστί are not annotated as targets.
Sentence 1: χαῖρε left unannotated: FrameNet 1.7 has no Greeting frame (χαίρω as an emotion verb, sentence 2 χαίρων, is annotated in Emotion_directed).
Sentence 1: φιλήσεαι (future middle with passive sense, 'you will be welcomed') is annotated in Have_visitor_over as a hospitality use of φιλέω (cf. Il. 3.207 ἐξείνισσα καὶ ἐν μεγάροισι φίλησα); the literal Experiencer_focus ('love') reading was rejected. παρ' ἄμμι ('at our house, among us') is taken as Host, because the frame has no location FE.
Sentence 1: δείπνου is annotated in Social_event (dinner.n); as the partitive object of πασσάμενος it is also the Ingestibles of Ingestion. The Social_event FE is Core-Unexpressed and not marked.
Sentence 1: in Telling, Topic and Medium are not marked as null instantiations (Topic is excluded when Message is expressed; Medium is not conceptually required for speech). In Needing, Consequences is not marked.
Sentence 2: ἐνὶ μεγάροις is marked as Host_location of ξεῖνον (Guest_and_host) but has no FE in the ξεινίσσομεν set, because Have_visitor_over has no location FE; the alternative frame Provide_lodging (Residence) would capture it.
Sentence 2: θεοῖσιν left unannotated: FrameNet 1.7 has no frame or LU for 'god/deity', and Rite has no FE for the divine recipient of a sacrifice, so the dative is not marked in the ἱερά set either.
Sentence 2: ῥέξομεν is treated as a support verb of ἱερά ('perform sacrifices') and not annotated separately; its unexpressed 1st-person-plural subject is the CNI Leader of the Rite set. ἱερά is marked both as target and as Type (rite named).
Sentence 2: for πομπή (both tokens) Sending is used (the host's conveyance of a guest home by ship); Recipient is not marked because no recipient distinct from the Goal is conceived. Transport_means is DNI (the Phaeacian ship).
Sentence 2: γέροντας is annotated in People_by_age (elderly.n); in context the γέροντες are the council of Phaeacian elders, a status sense that People_by_age does not capture (Leadership would).
Sentence 2: πόνου is annotated in Predicament ('trouble'); FrameNet has no LU for 'toil'. In Arriving two Manner FEs are marked (ἄνευθε πόνου καὶ ἀνίης; καρπαλίμως).
Sentence 2: ὥς ('so that') left unannotated: FrameNet 1.7 has no purpose-subordinator LU; μεσσηγύς ('in the meantime') is not a target (no LU) but is marked as Time in the πάθῃσι set; ὑφ' and ἐνί are left as parts of FE spans.
Sentence 2: πρίν (+ infinitive) and εἰ (καί) are subordinating conjunctions; the luName format has no conjunction tag, so both are given .prep as relators (after FrameNet before.prep in Time_vector and if.scon in Concessive). εἰ καί ('even if') is a two-word target.
Sentence 2: 'μηδέ' is one word in the text but two tokens (μη, δέ) in the token list; neither is a target.
Discontinuous FEs are not supported by the format: the Undesirable_event of πάθῃσι is marked as 'κακὸν καὶ πῆμα' without the quantifier τι, which is annotated separately as Evaluee of κακόν.
Null instantiations: CNI for pro-dropped subjects (Greek finite verbs and participles agreeing with an unexpressed subject), DNI for participants recoverable from context (Telemachus' household; Alcinous and the Phaeacians; Odysseus' homeland), INI for indefinite ones.
