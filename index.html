Homeric hospitality clusters · WordNet & FrameNet

The Ancient Greek hospitality WordNet & FrameNet experiment, redone with word lists from gloss-embedding clusters of Homeric lemmas. Each cluster was mapped to FrameNet twice: once without and once with the Ancient Greek WordNet layer, each run by a separate Claude Opus 5.5 agent. Every page below is browsable; the data files open on GitHub.
