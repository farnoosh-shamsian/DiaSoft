"At dawn we will call together more of the elders, entertain the stranger in the halls and perform fine sacrifices to the gods; and after that we will also turn our minds to his conveyance, so that under our escort the stranger may reach his native land without toil or distress, joyfully and quickly, even if he comes from very far away, and suffer no evil or harm on the way before he sets foot on his own land."

χαῖρε ‘hail! welcome!’ (Od. 1.123) left unannotated: FrameNet 1.7 has no Greeting frame (no LU for greet, hello, welcome or hail as a greeting).
θεοῖσιν ‘to the gods’ (Od. 7.190) left unannotated: FrameNet 1.7 has no frame with god.n or deity.n; the Rite frame has no FE for the deity who receives a sacrifice, so θεοῖσιν ‘to the gods’ is not given an FE in the ἱερά ‘sacrifices’ set either.
ῥέξομεν ‘we will offer (sacrifice)’ (Od. 7.191) is treated as a support verb of ἱερά ‘sacrifices’ (ῥέζειν ἱερά 'to perform sacrifices'); ἱερά ‘sacrifices’ is the target in Rite (sacrifice.n), and the unexpressed 1pl subject of the support verb is the Leader (CNI).
φιλήσεαι ‘you will be made welcome’ (Od. 1.123) is annotated in Guest_and_host for the Homeric hospitality sense of φιλέω ('be received kindly as a guest'); FrameNet has no LU for this sense. Treating_and_mistreating (treat.v) would be the fallback.
ξεινίσσομεν ‘we will entertain (as a guest)’ (Od. 7.190) is annotated in Guest_and_host, which has only noun LUs (guest.n, host.n); a verb LU is assumed on the analogy of befriend.v in Personal_relationship. The frame was chosen over Have_visitor_over because it has the Host_location FE that the Homeric phrase ἐν μεγάροισι ‘in the halls’ fills.
πομπή ‘escort, conveyance home’ (Od. 7.191, 7.193) is annotated in Sending. FrameNet has no frame for a host's conveyance of a departing guest; the escort component (Cotheme) and the fact that this conveyance is owed as hospitality are not represented.
Non-local FEs: in the sets for πομπῇ ‘by escort, by conveyance’, ἵκηται ‘may reach’ and χαίρων ‘rejoicing, glad’ the clause subject ὁ ξεῖνος ‘the stranger’ is marked as an overt FE (it is the conveyed Theme, the arriving Theme, and the controller of the participle). The Goal ἣν πατρίδα γαῖαν ‘his own native land’ is shared by the Sending (πομπῇ ‘by escort, by conveyance’) and Arriving (ἵκηται ‘may reach’) sets. In the γαῖα ‘land’ set πατρίδα ‘native, of one's fathers’ is treated as a Descriptor; Foreign_or_domestic_country (homeland.n) would be an alternative.
ἵκηται ‘may reach’ has two Depictive spans (ἄνευθε πόνου καὶ ἀνίης ‘without toil or distress’; χαίρων ‘rejoicing, glad’); the concessive clause εἰ καὶ μάλα τηλόθεν ἐστί ‘even if he is from very far away’ is marked as Circumstances, because Arriving has no Concessive FE.
δείπνου ‘of the meal’ is assigned to Food, because FrameNet has no meal.n LU. γέροντας ‘elders, old men’ is assigned to People_by_age, which covers 'old men' but not the institutional sense 'elders, council members'. τηλόθεν ('from afar') is reduced to distance in Gradable_proximity; its ablatival (source) meaning is lost. καρπαλίμως ‘swiftly’ is annotated in Taking_time (quickly.adv), with luName given in its adverbial form.
μεσσηγύς ‘in the meantime’ is marked only as the Time FE of πάθῃσι ‘he may suffer’, and κακόν ‘evil, harm’ only inside the Undesirable_event span. Neither is a separate target.
Function words (δέ ‘and, but’, καί ‘and; also, even’, ἠδέ ‘and’, αὐτάρ ‘but, and then’, ὡς ‘so that; as’, ἄν ‘modal particle (would)’, εἰ ‘if’, μή ‘not’, γε ‘at least, indeed’, the article), pronouns, possessive adjectives (ἡμέτερος ‘our’, ἑός ‘his own’), the copula ἐστί ‘is’ and the prepositions (παρά ‘beside; from; at the house of’, ἐν ‘in’, ἐπί ‘on; in addition’, περί ‘about, concerning’, ὑπό ‘under; by’, ἄνευθε ‘without’) are not separate targets. Prepositions are included in the spans of the FEs they head.
Null instantiations: pro-dropped and controlled subjects are CNI. Only conceptually required core FEs are marked as null instantiations. Where FrameNet groups FEs in a core set (Host/Host_location, Sender/Transport_means, Recipient/Goal, Whole/Relative_time, Dependent/Consequences), only one member is marked. FEs incorporated in the target (e.g. Age for γέροντας ‘elders, old men’) are not marked.
