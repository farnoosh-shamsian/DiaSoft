Proposed frames (full definitions)

🆕 Guest_friendship

Two individuals, Partner_1 and Partner_2 (jointly Partners), usually heads of households belonging to different communities, are bound by a lasting, reciprocal relationship of hospitality: each is obliged to receive, feed, protect, honour with gifts and send on the other (and often the other's descendants) whenever he comes to his community. The bond is typically founded or renewed by an exchange of Tokens (guest-gifts), may be inherited (Source_of_relationship), and is held to be under divine Sanction. Unlike Guest_and_host, the relation is symmetric and outlasts any single visit; unlike Personal_relationship, it is defined by mutual obligations of hospitality rather than by affection or kinship.

Inherits from Personal_relationship; uses Guest_and_host and Giving.

Core frame elements
Partner_1One party to the guest-friendship, typically expressed as subject, possessor or dative of reference.
Partner_2The other party; with the relational noun ξένος 'guest-friend' it is the referent of the noun itself.
PartnersBoth parties expressed together, e.g. as a plural subject or with a reciprocal pronoun.
Selected non-core FEs
TokenA gift exchanged to found, seal or later prove the bond (ξεινήιον, ξείνια).
Source_of_relationshipWhat the bond derives from, e.g. inheritance from fathers (πατρώϊος) or an earlier act of hospitality.
DurationHow long the bond has existed (e.g. παλαιός 'of long standing').
DescriptorA characterization of the bond (e.g. προσκηδής 'caring, affectionate').
SanctionThe deity or norm that guarantees the bond (e.g. Ζεὺς ξείνιος, θέμις).
PlaceThe community or house in which the obligations of the bond are exercised.
Lexical units
ξενοσύνηNguest-friendship (the bond itself)
ξένοςNguest-friend (the partner; relational noun)
ξενίαNguest-friendship, friendly relation
ξεινήιονNgift of guest-friendship (denotes the Token)

🆕 Tending

A Caretaker gives sustained attention and work to a Ward (a person, a person's life or livelihood, a household, animals, plants or other property) so as to keep it in good condition or meet its needs, often on behalf of a Principal who owns or is responsible for the Ward. Unlike Assistance, no particular Goal of a Benefited_party is profiled; unlike Subordinates_and_superiors, no relation of command is required; unlike Growing_food, the Ward need not be a crop.

Inherits from Intentionally_act; uses Assistance.

Core frame elements
CaretakerThe person who does the tending.
WardThe person, livelihood, animal, plant or property that is tended.
Selected non-core FEs
PrincipalThe person on whose behalf or for whose benefit the Ward is tended, e.g. its owner.
MannerThe way the tending is carried out (e.g. well, carefully).
DurationHow long the tending lasts.
PlaceWhere the tending takes place.
TimeWhen the tending takes place.
PurposeWhat the Caretaker intends to achieve by the tending.
Lexical units
