Proposed frames (full definitions)

🆕 Guest_friendship

Two Partners (Partner_1 and Partner_2, or jointly Partners), normally members of different communities, are bound in a lasting and reciprocal relationship of ritualised friendship (Greek xenia). The bond is typically created when one Partner receives the other as a guest and the two exchange gifts (Token); it outlasts the visit, may be inherited by the Partners' descendants (Origin_of_bond), and obliges each Partner to receive, protect and help the other or the other's kin whenever they come, under the sanction of a Guarantor deity. Unlike Guest_and_host, the relationship is not temporary and both Partners can play either role; unlike Personal_relationship, it is defined by hospitality and gift exchange rather than by affection or kinship.

Inherits from Personal_relationship; uses Guest_and_host and Exchange.

Core frame elements
Partner_1One of the two guest-friends, when the Partners are expressed separately.
Partner_2The other guest-friend, when the Partners are expressed separately.
PartnersBoth guest-friends expressed together (often with a reciprocal pronoun).
Selected non-core FEs
Origin_of_bondHow or through whom the bond arose, e.g. inherited from the Partners' fathers (πατρώιος ‘of the fathers, inherited’) or begun by a particular visit.
TokenThe gift(s) exchanged that create, renew or attest the bond (ξεινήιον ‘guest-gift’, δῶρα ‘gifts’).
GuarantorThe deity under whose protection the bond stands (Ζεὺς ξείνιος ‘Zeus, protector of guests’).
DurationHow long the bond has lasted (παλαιός 'of old').
MannerThe quality of the bond (προσκηδής 'affectionate, caring').

🆕 Tending

A Caretaker attends, on an ongoing basis, to the needs or condition of a Charge — a person, animal, plant, piece of land, household or livelihood — so as to keep it in good condition or improve its well-being. The Caretaker is often a servant or dependant acting on behalf of an Owner of the Charge. The frame differs from Assistance (help towards a specific Goal of a Benefited_party), from Agriculture (cultivation of Food) and from Cure (healing an Affliction) in profiling continuous care and upkeep rather than a goal, a crop or a cure.

Inherits from Intentionally_act; uses Assistance.

Core frame elements
CaretakerThe person who looks after the Charge.
ChargeThe person, animal, plant, land, household or livelihood that is looked after.

PurposeWhat the Caretaker aims to achieve by the tending.
