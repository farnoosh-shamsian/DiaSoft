🆕 Guest_friendship

Two individuals (or their houses) belonging to different communities, the Guest_friends (or Guest_friend_1 and Guest_friend_2), are bound by a lasting, reciprocal and often hereditary relationship of hospitality. The bond is typically initiated when one party is received in the other's house and is sealed by an exchange of Tokens (guest-gifts); it obliges each party, and often their descendants, to receive, protect and give gifts to the other whenever he comes, and it may be sanctioned by a divine Guarantor. Unlike Guest_and_host, the relationship outlasts any single visit and is symmetrical: each party is potentially both host and guest.

Inherits from Relation_between_individuals; uses Guest_and_host and Exchange and Giving.

Core frame elements
Guest_friend_1One party to the bond, typically the one in focus (e.g. the referent of ξένος ‘guest, stranger; guest-friend’).
Guest_friend_2The other party to the bond, to whom Guest_friend_1 is a guest-friend.
Guest_friendsBoth parties expressed together (e.g. as a plural subject or with a reciprocal).
Selected non-core FEs
TokenThe gift(s) exchanged that establish or attest the bond (ξεινήια ‘guest-gifts; hospitable fare’, δῶρα ‘gifts’).
Origin_eventThe reception or meeting at which the bond began.
GuarantorThe deity who sanctions the bond and punishes its violation (Ζεὺς ξείνιος ‘Zeus, protector of guests’).
DurationHow long the bond has lasted, including its hereditary standing (παλαιός ‘old, of long standing’).
DescriptorA characterization of the bond or of a party as a guest-friend (πατρώϊος 'ancestral', προσκηδής 'caring').

🆕 Honorific_prerogative

A Grantor (a community, an army in assembly, a leader or a host) allots to a Recipient a Prerogative: a material share set apart from what is common (a prize from the spoils, a choice portion of meat, a funeral mound) or a standing privilege, as public acknowledgment of the Recipient's status, age or merit (the Basis). Holding the Prerogative constitutes honour; being without it is public dishonour. Unlike Rewards_and_punishments, no judgment of a specific action is presupposed, and unlike plain Giving the transfer is evaluative and status-marking.

uses Giving and Possession and Judgment and Rewards_and_punishments.

Core frame elements
RecipientThe person honoured with (or lacking) the Prerogative.
PrerogativeThe honorific share, prize or privilege.
GrantorThe person or group that allots the Prerogative.
Selected non-core FEs
BasisThe status, age, rank or merit for which the Prerogative is due.
OccasionThe event at which it is allotted (division of spoils, sacrifice, funeral).
MannerHow the allotment is made.
TimeWhen the allotment is made.

🆕 Plaything

An object, the Plaything, is made, kept or given for the amusement of a User (prototypically a child) rather than for practical use. It is valued for the Delight it affords and is often a small, attractive object (a trinket) that may be given as a present or traded.

Inherits from Artifact; uses Stimulus_focus.
